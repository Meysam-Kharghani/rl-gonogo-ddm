## Interpretation constraints
- Repeated outer partitions reuse the same 40 participants and are stability analyses, not independent replications.
- Positive predictive contrasts do not identify a unique causal or cognitive mechanism.
- The 700-ms value is an analytic observation horizon, not a verified task-enforced response deadline.
- The historical first-passage policy increment is concentrated mainly in censored/survival contributions rather than observed-response density.
